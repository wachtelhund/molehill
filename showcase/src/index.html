Molehill

Dig a holt in the endless Deepholt.

Concept illustration, not gameplay — not a screenshot of the game.

You play a Delver — a mole who digs, props, and furnishes a home in an infinite body of earth. There is no sky and no surface. Sand pours, water finds its level, and a roof that spans too far comes down. Nothing here is magic. The Lid, an old story about open air far above, has never been confirmed.

This page is a look at the work. There is no game to play in the browser, and downloads will come later.

The ground

Carve a way that will hold

You cut a round bite through dirt and stone, then run, cling, and wall-jump through the tunnels you made. What you leave behind has to carry its own weight. A short span of dirt holds; a wide hall needs pillars. Brick and iron are ordinary cells, judged by the same numbers as the rock.

The Deepholt has no top and no bottom. Higher up it is cooler and wetter. Further down it is hotter, harder, and richer. You start in the middle, with a small burrow, and dig.

Physics

Weight, water, fire

Every cell is a material with density and strength. Sand slides. Water levels out. Lava creeps, and where it meets water it quenches to loose scoria. A charge is a pressure wave: it roars down a tunnel, fades across a cave, and breaks dirt long before granite. When stone cracks, it crumbles into the falling-sand sim, and the next span feels the load.

The holt

Make the dark feel like home

A holt is a colony. You furnish it — tables, nests, rugs, carved pillars, painted stone — and keep it lit with handmade lamps. The work is quiet: dig out, bring things home, prop the roof, light another stretch of wall.

New Holt asks how you want to live. Survival starts with a small kit. You dig with the tools you own, find food, and burn fungus oil for light. When you fall, the pack stays where you died. Creative has every item, and nothing hurts.

The Deepholt

No sky, ever

Nobody has found a top or a bottom. You wander, dig, and listen. Older Delvers left marks in the rock that seem to point upward. They prove nothing. The Lid remains a myth, and the game never shows it.

There is no day cycle and no sky light. A tunnel stays dark until you bring a lamp, a torch, or the faint glow of fungus and crystal. Delvers see poorly. They rely on light, touch, and vibration.
