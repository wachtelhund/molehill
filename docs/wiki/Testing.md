# Testing

> Full reference: [`TESTING.md`](../../TESTING.md) in the repository root. This page explains the idea and the workflow.

## Why the game can be tested without a screen

Every gameplay rule lives in `molehill-sim`, which has **no Bevy dependency**: no window, no GPU, no audio. The game crate only turns key presses into an `Input` struct and draws what the sim produces. So a test, or an AI agent, can drive the exact same `World::step()` the player does, and check the result as numbers.

The sim is also **deterministic**. Randomness is a hash of position and tick, the parallel falling-sand phases can't race, and disasters use their own random stream. The same scenario always gives the same output, so a failure is reproducible rather than flaky.

```
 .scn file ─▶ stamp map over a generated world ─▶ hold keys / mine / place / wire per tick ─▶ World::step() × N
                                                                              │
                                     PASS / FAIL lines, JSON dumps, ASCII maps ◀─ expect <var> <op> <n>
```

## Scenarios

A `.scn` file is a tiny, line-based script. An ASCII map is stamped over a generated world with `@` on the spawn point, a 5 m granite frame keeps the world out, and disasters are off. Scenarios are written in **tiles of 0.25 m**: each map character is a 2 × 2 block of cells, and offsets, radii, positions, speeds and counts are all in tiles, so a scenario means the same at any resolution:

```
// a full jump clears 4.5 tiles and lands
map
##########
#........#
#.@......#
##########
end
hold 1-30 right
hold 31-40 right jump
expect 60 rise >= 4.5
expect 120 grounded == 1
```

* **Directives** drive the mole and the world: `hold` keys over a tick range, `mine` / `dig` a cell, `tool` and `brush` to pick a tool and size, `place` / `put` blocks, `device` / `use` / `pickup` / `wire` for copperline and devices, `explode T dx dy kg` (a blast of kg TNT-equivalent), `give` items, `set` a vital, a pack count (`set T inv:Drill 1`) or the quest (`set T quest N`) mid-run, `undiscovered` to make a material count as not yet seen (its first sight then logs a discovery), `place` a cart, ladder, lift or tube hopper and `load` a cart (transport; maps lay rail with `=` and pressure tube with `%`), `saveload` to round-trip the world mid-run, `slide` to send the scenario's rows into stored bands and back, and `throw` / `drop` to launch or place a loose item, and the light directives: `lamp` (headlamp on or off; off by default in scenarios), `aim` (where the mole looks, so where the beam points), `wield` (what it holds; a torch lights) and `torch` (put a torch on a tile). `grotto T dx dy rx ry` sets a grotto's glowmite swarm over a tile, and `catch T dx dy` catches one there. For brushes and building: `hand` (the brush item in hand), `sizekey` / `sizewheel` (resize it as the keys or the wheel would), `snap` (grid snap on or off) and `line` (drag a snapped line of blocks). `mode survival` or `mode creative` starts the scenario in a game mode with its rules and pack (scenarios run in the bare **Plain** mode otherwise). For crafting and the holt: `switch` (change mode mid-run), `craft` (make a recipe as at a station), `station` (build one on a tile), `pour` (set a tile's material, such as lava beside a forge), `furnish` (set down furniture) and `paint` (paint blocks or dye cloth with a pigment). `clue` puts a Lid clue (Carving, Note, Draft, Relic) on a tile's floor. `ruin` stamps a ruin of the Ancients (`hall`, `vault`, or `tube`, with `left` for a tube running left) with its top-left on a tile; a vault's chest is registered. `wall` sets a tile's back lining, `scrape` takes lining off, and `site` stamps a found place (`mine`, `holt`, `pump`, `fissure`, `fossil`, `crystal`, `halt`). Expect `wall:DX,DY,Kind`, `walls:Kind`, `sites` and `sites_seen`.
* **Assertions** (`expect T <var> <op> <n>`) read the player (`px`, `vx`, `rise`, `grounded`, `cling`, `hp`, `flash`…), survival state (`hunger`, `oil` seconds of headlamp oil left, `lamp`, `mode`, `hungeron`), the message log (`log:WORD` lines containing WORD, `_` for a space), furnishing (`furn:Item` how many stand, `tint:Item` the first one's dye, `paint:DX,DY` the pigment on a tile), the quest chain (`quest`, the current quest's index), the Lid thread (`journal` entries read: clue fragments and ruin carvings, `clues` unread clues in the map, `clues_bands:A,B` how many clues the generator places in 32 m bands A..B), the Ancients (`relics` a bit mask of relics found: 1 the Ancient bit, 2 the Ground lens; `ruins_seen` ruins whose carving was read), the map (`count:Water`, `static:WoodBeam`, `is:DX,DY,Mat`, `util:DX,DY` stress utilisation, `strain:DX,DY` the hover strain level, `strain` how many strained roof cells the mole's strain watch sees, `awake` chunks), devices (`dev:`, `on:`, `lit:`, `wire:`) the brush (`brush`, its radius in tiles; `snapcells`, the snapped square's side), loose items (`items:Stone`, position and speed `ix:` `iy:` `ivx:` `ivy:`, `irest:` asleep, `ilit:` a lit charge), light (`light:DX,DY`, the brightness at a tile: about 1 is fully lit, under 0.05 is dark), `glowmites` (how many drift over the map), `p:DX,DY` (the last blast's overpressure on a tile), blast debris and the wave (`debris`, `debris:Mat`, `wave:DX,DY`, `front`, `frontcells`), transport (carts `carts`, `cartx`, `cartv`, `cargo`, `cartrail`, `cartpow`, `riding`; ladders and lifts `climbing`, `ladder_up` / `ladder_down` (the game's `W` / `S` would climb the rope here, not jump / dig), `lifty`, `liftv`, `ballast`; tubes `hoppers`, `capsules`, `intube`, `pressure`) chests opened (`chests`) and events (`ev:Explode`).
* **Output** is easy to grep: `PASS` / `FAIL` per file, one `FAIL <file>:<line> tick T: …` per broken assertion, optional `DUMP` JSON and `ASCII` maps, and a final `N scenarios, K failed` (exit code 1 on failure).

The variables, the map legend and the list of every included scenario are in [`TESTING.md`](../../TESTING.md).

## Writing one (the loop for agents)

1. Copy the closest `.scn` and draw the map. Leave 3 empty tiles above `@` for the mole's body.
2. Add `dump` / `ascii` lines at the ticks you care about and run that file alone. Read the JSON to choose thresholds, for example the tick where `grounded` flips.
3. Turn what you saw into `expect` lines and remove the noisy dumps.
4. Run the whole folder.

Feel changes are tested the same way: rise height, `vx` after N ticks, the coyote window, and how many ticks a mid-air turn takes. Every refinement run in the history added scenarios for what it changed (for example `hall_sag_arrests`, `player_lava_sink`, `tnt_thrum`, `shaft_jump`). Generated features are tested by stamping them into a scenario map. The ruins are the newest example: `ruin_hall_stands` (a pumping hall stands 20 s under an open cave), `ruin_hall_caves_in` (the same hall under 3 m of sand: its brick bays crack and fall in, while the iron holds) and `ruin_vault_loot` (tunnel into three vaults with a Pickaxe: the Ancient bit, then the Ground lens, then only old materials; each carving joins the journal once; a save keeps both relics and the journal). Each found place also has a walk: `site_*_traverse` goes in from a way in and reaches the chest (or, for the pumpworks, fossil cave and fissure, a named point short of a hazard its header describes). Transport has one scenario per behaviour: carts (`cart_rolls_downhill`, `cart_push_level`, `cart_load_heavier`, `cart_ride`, `cart_cargo`, `cart_off_rail`, `rail_needs_support`), powered rails (`powered_rail_climbs`, `rail_too_steep_to_push`), ladders and lifts (`rope_ladder_climb`, `rope_ladder_top_out`, `rope_ladder_down`, `rope_ladder_let_go`, `rope_ladder_falls`, `lift_counterweight`, `lift_powered`) and tubes (`tube_sends_items`, `tube_breach`, `tube_rides_mole`). The production line has one per machine: `chute_slides`, `cart_tips_at_bumper`, `ore_hopper_feeds_cart` and `shaker_sorts`, plus `cart_tip_resumes` and the end-to-end `production_line`. Every found-place family has a walk-through, `site_<kind>_traverse` (holt, pumpworks, mine, fissure, fossil, crystal, halt): from a way in along the critical path as far as the stamped layout allows, with the known blockers written in each. Blasts in granite are checked before and after the granite pass by `blast_granite_chips`, `blast_granite_pocket` and `blast_granite_vs_dirt`.

## Looking at it

Visual checks are optional and come last. `cargo build --release -p molehill`, then:

* `molehill --fresh --seed=4242 --shot=out.bmp [--shot-at=150] [--shot-ui=pause|inv|help|dev] [--shot-walk] [--shot-blast=K] [--shot-zoom=0.6]` starts a holt, writes one frame and quits;
* `--scene=mine|holt|pump|fissure|fossil|crystal|halt|walls` stamps that found place (or a mixed lining room) in host rock next to the burrow so a still can inspect it without hunting a seed; `--scene=water|tnt|lore|decor|wiring|production` are the older set pieces (`production`: a chute onto a shaker screen, an ore hopper of copper ore over track and a bumper);
* `--dump-art=DIR` also writes every sprite at 8× (icons, devices, furnishings, furniture, the mole) and the composited world around the mole: `view.bmp` with every visible world sprite on it, `view_cells.bmp` the cells alone. It is all CPU-side, so it works even where the window can't be captured, and it renders the whole window, not just the part on screen.

On macOS the window has to be visible on screen to capture a frame. On headless Linux, use Xvfb with the lavapipe Vulkan driver. One screenshot is enough, and only for visual changes.
